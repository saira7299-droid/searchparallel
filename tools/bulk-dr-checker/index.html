❓ Frequently Asked Questions
What is Domain Rating (DR)?
Domain Rating (DR) is a metric by Ahrefs that measures the strength of a website's backlink profile on a scale from 0 to 100. Higher DR means more authoritative sites are linking to that domain. It is one of the most trusted indicators of a website's overall link authority in the SEO industry.
Is this tool completely free? Do I need an Ahrefs account?
Yes — 100% free, forever. Ahrefs publicly released their Domain Rating endpoint with no API key requirement. You do not need an Ahrefs account, subscription, or credit card. The data is fetched directly from Ahrefs' public API.
How accurate is the DR data?
The data comes directly from Ahrefs' own public API, so it is the exact same DR score shown inside the Ahrefs platform. It is updated regularly as Ahrefs crawls the web.
Why is DR useful for local SEO?
DR helps you quickly assess the link-building value of local directories, citation sites, news sites, and competitor domains. A backlink from a DR 60 site carries far more SEO weight than one from DR 10. Use this tool to prioritise which sites to target for local link building.
How many domains can I check and how long does it take?
You can check up to 100 domains per batch. Each domain is fetched with a short delay to respect Ahrefs' rate limits. Results appear live as they load — 10 domains takes about 5 seconds, 100 domains about 40–50 seconds.
